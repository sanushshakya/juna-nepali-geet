About

A small, free corner of the internet for the songs many of us grew up with. No sign-up, no song list, no searching: press play and let them roll.

Made just for fun, for the love of the music. Nothing is sold and there are no ads. Visits are counted with a simple visitor counter and Cloudflare Web Analytics, and neither uses cookies.

Songs stream from official YouTube uploads. No audio is hosted here. All rights belong to the artists and labels.

Visitor count: one anonymous hit per browser via abacus.jasoncameron.dev, no cookies.

Traffic statistics: Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not fingerprint visitors.
